Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to authenticate you, to save and display your expenses, and to produce summaries and filters of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to retain it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may make requests to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add services that handle your data in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
